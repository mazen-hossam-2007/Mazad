📖 OFFICIAL MAZAD HOSSAM RULEBOOK

HOW TO PLAY MAZAD HOSSAM

👥

1. The Setup & Starting XI

Two managers compete to assemble a complete 11-player squad on the tactical pitch, starting from Goalkeeper up to Striker. Both start with an equal transfer budget (default: €200M).

⚡

2. Strict Turn-Based Bidding

Auctions are strictly turn-based. Only the active manager can place a bid (+1M, +5M, +10M, +20M, ALL IN) or Pass. There is a natural pause as managers react. The inactive player's panel is dimmed.

🎁

3. The Lucky Draw Rule

When a manager passes, the other manager wins the auction player and pays their winning bid. The losing manager does NOT leave empty-handed: they instantly receive a free surprise player of the exact same position via the weighted Lucky Draw system (with chances for insane superstars, world-class gems, or unfortunate flops)!

💰

4. Budget Discipline

You must fill all 11 positions. If you blow all your money in round 3, you'll be forced to rely entirely on free Lucky Draw rewards for the remaining positions.

🏟️

5. Match Day Simulation

Once both squads are complete, they clash in a live 90-minute simulation with live commentary, goals, saves, cards, and an MVP crowning. Team performance is governed by sector ratings (Attack, Midfield, Defense, Goalkeeping), formation fit, and chemistry.
